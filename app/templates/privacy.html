Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how InstaTone handles information when you use the website and its audio-processing features.

1. Information We Process

When you use the ringtone maker, you may upload audio or video files or submit media URLs for processing. Files are temporarily stored on the server only while the requested ringtone is being created and are automatically deleted afterwards.

2. Uploaded Audio and Video

Audio and video files are used solely to provide the ringtone creation service you request. Users must not upload content that they are not authorized to process. InstaTone does not retain or share uploaded media files.

3. Generated Ringtone Files

Generated ringtone files are temporarily stored on the server to allow you to download them. They are automatically deleted from the server after a short period.

4. Cookies and Analytics

InstaTone may use essential cookies for website functionality and analytics tools to understand aggregate usage. We do not sell personal data.

5. Advertising

InstaTone may use third-party advertising networks (such as Monetag or Adsterra) to display ads. These networks may use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own privacy policies. You can refer to their documentation for opt-out options.

6. Third-Party Services

InstaTone uses third-party services for hosting, infrastructure, and analytics. These services may process limited technical information such as IP addresses and browser identifiers in accordance with their own privacy policies.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect data handled by the service. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security, and users upload media at their own risk.

8. Children's Privacy

InstaTone is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, services, or applicable legal requirements change. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us via the Contact page.
