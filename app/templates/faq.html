Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about using InstaTone to create custom MP3 and iPhone ringtones.

What is InstaTone?

InstaTone is a free online ringtone maker. Upload or link any audio/video and trim the exact section you want. Export it as MP3 for Android or M4R for iPhone, with optional fade-in and fade-out effects.

Which file formats are supported?

InstaTone supports MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC audio files, as well as MP4, MOV, WEBM, and MKV video files. You can also paste media URLs from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Vimeo, and other supported platforms.

What output formats can I download?

MP3 — for Android, Windows and Mac (maximum 60 seconds).
M4R — native Apple iPhone ringtone format (maximum 40 seconds, enforced by iOS).

How do I set a ringtone on iPhone?

Select M4R format when creating your ringtone. Once downloaded, you can set it via: (1) Apple GarageBand app → Share as Ringtone, or (2) Sync via Finder (Mac) / iTunes (Windows) under the Tones tab. See our step-by-step guide.

How do I set a ringtone on Android?

Download the MP3 file, then go to Settings → Sound & Vibration → Phone Ringtone → Add Ringtone, and select your downloaded file.

What are the Fade In / Fade Out effects?

Enabling these applies a 1.5-second smooth fade at the beginning and end of your ringtone, preventing harsh audio pops when your phone rings.

Can I use YouTube or Instagram links?

Yes. Paste a public YouTube, Instagram, TikTok or Vimeo link into the URL tab. InstaTone will extract the audio stream. Only use links to content you own or are authorized to process.

Can I use copyrighted music?

Only process audio that you own or are legally authorized to use. Copyright restrictions apply to commercial music and other protected content. You are solely responsible for the content you submit.

Is InstaTone free?

Yes — 100% free with no registration, no watermarks, and no hidden fees.
