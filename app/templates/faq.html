Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about using InstaTone to create ringtones from audio files.

What is InstaTone?

InstaTone is an online ringtone maker that lets you select a portion of an audio file and create an MP3 ringtone.

Which audio files are supported?

InstaTone currently supports MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC and several common video formats including MP4, MOV, WEBM and MKV.

How long can my ringtone be?

The current InstaTone ringtone maker limits the generated ringtone to a maximum of 60 seconds.

What format is the ringtone?

The generated ringtone is provided as an MP3 audio file.

Do I need to install software?

No. InstaTone is designed to work through a web browser.

Can I use copyrighted music?

Only process audio that you own or are legally authorized to use. Copyright restrictions may apply to commercial music and other protected content.

Is InstaTone responsible for the audio I upload?

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights or permissions to process the audio they submit.

Is InstaTone free?

The current ringtone maker is designed as a free tool. Future features or services may have different terms.
