Frequently Asked Questions

What files can I use?

InstaTone supports common audio and video formats such as MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, MP4, MOV, WEBM and MKV.

Can I choose the ringtone section?

Yes. Use the waveform selection handles to choose the start and end of the ringtone.

What format is the result?

The current ringtone output is MP3.
