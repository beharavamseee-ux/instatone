Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set an M4R ringtone on my iPhone?

Download the M4R file. You can transfer it directly to your iPhone using Finder (Mac) or iTunes (Windows) under the "Tones" section, or save it to iCloud Files and set it via the free Apple GarageBand app.

What is the maximum ringtone length for iPhone?

Apple iOS restricts ringtones to a maximum duration of 40 seconds. InstaTone automatically enforces this limit when M4R format is selected.

Why do fade in and fade out effects matter?

Fading in and out softens the beginning and end of the audio, preventing abrupt clicks and harsh cutoff noises when an incoming call alerts you.
